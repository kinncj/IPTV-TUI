# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, whatever their age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Standards

Things that help:

- Be respectful of differing opinions and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

Things that are not acceptable:

- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying these standards and may remove,
edit, or reject contributions that do not follow them, and may ban contributors
for behavior they consider inappropriate.

Report unacceptable behavior by opening a GitHub issue at
https://github.com/kinncj/IPTV-TUI/issues. Include what happened and any
evidence, such as screenshots or links. The maintainer will review each report
and respond as they judge appropriate.
