# Content and legal notice

IPTV TUI hosts nothing and distributes nothing. It is a browser and a launcher
for playlists that already exist, maintained by community projects that this
project is not affiliated with. Opening a channel here is the same as opening
those public lists in VLC yourself.

It reads public M3U playlists and hands a stream URL to a media player you
already have installed (mpv, vlc, or ffplay), or decodes it for the built-in
inline player with ffmpeg. No stream is stored, cached as video, or rebroadcast.

## Where the lists come from

Every playlist shipped with the app comes from two independent, community-run
projects:

- iptv-org/iptv: https://github.com/iptv-org/iptv
- Free-TV/IPTV: https://github.com/Free-TV/IPTV

The app can also load playlists you add yourself through the sources manager or a
config file. Those are entirely your own.
